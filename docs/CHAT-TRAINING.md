Thank you so much for thinking of us for your project. At the moment we aren't taking on client builds or renovations, as our focus is solely on designing and building our own residences.

**Do you do renovations or knockdown rebuilds?**
Thank you for thinking of us. At the moment we aren't taking on client builds or renovations, as our focus is solely on designing and building our own residences.

**Can I engage you for design only?**
Design, development and construction happen together at Sabdia, one team on its own residences, so design and interiors are not offered as a separate service.

**I am a buyer's agent**
Leave your details, your agency and the residence, and a member of our team will be in touch directly.

**I am a real estate agent**
Agents can apply for access at sabdia.com.au/pages/agent-access, and our team will be in touch.

**I am a tradesperson or supplier**
Sabdia manages its own construction, so trades and suppliers work with our team directly. Leave your details, your trade and where you work, and the construction team will be in touch when there is a fit.

**Are you hiring?**
Send your details and the role you are interested in through sabdia.com.au/pages/contact, and it will reach the right person.

**I am from the media**
Send your details and your story through sabdia.com.au/pages/contact, and a member of our team will be in touch.
