## The voice in one line

Restrained, editorial, architectural. Sabdia describes the home; it never sells it, argues for it, or talks about other builders. The reader draws the conclusion.

## Five words

Considered · Restrained · Confident · Material · Timeless

## Vocabulary

**Rising in the corpus, use freely:** natural · stone · travertine · warm timber · layered · calm · light · restraint · proportion · resolved · considered · refined · cohesive · timeless · deeply liveable · grounded · sun-washed · honed · vein-cut · hand-laid · crazy pave · Venetian plaster · brushed brass / copper · arched · sculptural (about a stair or a form, not as a claim)

**Receding, use sparingly and never as the point:** luxury (noun is fine in the descriptor; "luxurious" as an adjective is not) · modern · bespoke · city · views

**Never:** premium · high-end · stunning · gorgeous · world-class · cutting-edge · affordable · "buy now" · "limited time" · "don't miss" · "no shortcuts" (a claim, not a description) · "good enough"

**Say residence, not property.** Say "the studio" or "Sabdia" for the company. Say "one team" and "in-house" for the model.

## The shape of a residence description

The caption formula, confirmed across all 154, translates straight to the site:

1. **Place it.** Name, suburb, what it is. *"Set high in the hills of Taringa, AMMOS brings a Mediterranean-inspired presence to one of Brisbane's most established inner-west suburbs."*
2. **The material paragraph.** The longest, and the one that persuades. Name what is there: *"Natural stone, warm timber, Venetian plaster and brushed copper establish a grounded palette that carries without compromise, from the facade through to the master dressing room."*
3. **How it is lived in.** Rooms, light, flow. Specific, not listed: *"Nine metres of sliding glass dissolve the boundary between interior and landscape."*
4. **A quiet close.** One line naming the value behind the work. Never an argument, never an aphorism about work or process. *"Every junction, every transition, carefully resolved."*

Length: real 2026 captions average 548 characters. A residence page description runs two to four short paragraphs in that register.

## What it is not

The site had drifted into a plain contractor register: "Beautiful homes, built properly", "no shortcuts", "You see every stage", "If something needs attention, we come back", "we would not cut corners". Honest, but not Sabdia. The corpus never reassures and never promises; it shows the stone.

It is also not the essayist register: cleverness about photography, aphorisms about work ("the quietest week of a build"), artisan romance. Naomi, 13 Aug 2026: "these aren't Sabdia brand tone." The account describes the home, not the making of it as a story.
