# SABDIA — How to Write for the Website

Naomi's guide, 14 Sep 2026 (from `~/Downloads/SABDIA-voice-guide.md.pdf`).
Use this when writing or reviewing any copy for the SABDIA website. If
you're using an AI tool, paste this whole thing in as the instructions
first, then give it the section you want written.

Site-only addition: no em dashes on the website (use a comma, a full stop
or a colon); the guide's own examples use them, the site does not.

## Who we are

SABDIA is a luxury residential design and construction studio in Brisbane.
We design and build bespoke high-end homes, from Mediterranean modern to
contemporary Queenslander. Every project is considered down to the finish.

## The voice in one line

**Clean, professional, restrained: outstanding without shouting about it.**
Never celebrity-style, never salesy. Quiet luxury: the confidence is in the
detail, not the volume.

## Say it like this

- Lead with **materials and craft**, not adjectives. Name the travertine,
  the limewash, the aged brass, the light oak joinery. Specifics feel luxe;
  vague hype feels cheap.
- **Warm, organic, sculptural.** Our world is soft light, natural stone,
  honest timber, considered proportion.
- Use **bespoke terminology**: bespoke, crafted, considered, curated,
  tailored, commissioned.
- Write in **calm, confident sentences.** A little space and restraint
  reads as expensive.

## Don't

- **No item counts or quantities in styling/spec copy.** Not "5 bedrooms,
  3 bathrooms, 2 living areas." Describe the experience of the spaces
  instead. (Numbers live in the listing spec sheet, not the storytelling
  copy.)
- No hype words: stunning, luxurious, dream home, must-see, unbeatable,
  one-of-a-kind, jaw-dropping. If it belongs in a real-estate hard-sell,
  cut it.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- Don't over-explain. Trust the reader.

## Our vocabulary (pull from here)

- **Materials and finishes:** travertine, limewash, aged/antique brass,
  light timber, oak joinery, natural stone, honed surfaces
- **Feel:** warm, organic, sculptural, considered, restrained, quiet
  luxury, tactile, timeless, refined
- **Craft:** bespoke, crafted, commissioned, curated, tailored, detailed,
  seamless joinery
- **Spaces:** loggia, alfresco, arrival, entertaining zone, private wing,
  retreat

## Before → After

- ✗ "This stunning 5-bedroom dream home has luxurious finishes throughout!"
  ✓ "A home shaped around light and stone: travertine underfoot, oak
  joinery, and a loggia made for slow afternoons."
- ✗ "High-quality kitchen with premium appliances and lots of storage."
  ✓ "A quietly generous kitchen: honed stone, integrated joinery, and
  finishes chosen to age beautifully."
- ✗ "We build amazing luxury homes for our clients."
  ✓ "We design and build bespoke homes: considered, warm, and crafted to
  last."

*When in doubt: would this sound at home in a restrained, high-end design
studio, or in a loud real-estate ad? Aim for the studio.*

## Where the numbers live on the site

Bed, bath, garage and land counts print from the product metafields on the
cards, the facts rows and the Key Features list. The description field is
storytelling copy and carries none.
